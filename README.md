# F13LD.vault

A community-powered metamaterial structure library and recommendation engine. Describe your application requirements — the tool finds the geometry.

**[Open f13ld.vault](https://mshomper.github.io/f13ld.vault)**

---

## What It Is

field.vault is a searchable database of metamaterial designs generated by the [f13ld.sweep tool](https://mshomper.github.io/f13ld.sweep). Each record contains full mechanical and thermal homogenization results alongside the complete implicit geometry definition.

The goal is simple: an engineer who needs a bone scaffold, a heat exchanger, or a lightweight aerospace component shouldn't need to understand TPMS mathematics. They describe what they need. f13ld.vault finds the structure.

---

## How to Use

1. Select your **application domain** — Biomedical, Aerospace, Automotive, Oil & Gas, or Thermal
2. Set **target requirements** — metric ranges relevant to your application (microstrain, pore size, anisotropy, etc.)
3. Adjust **importance weights** for each requirement
4. Click **Find Structures** — results are ranked by match score
5. Click any result to inspect the full metric set and preview the surface geometry
6. Click **Get Recipe JSON** to download the design file
7. Load the recipe into the companion tools at [F13LD](f13ld.app) and export to 3MF
