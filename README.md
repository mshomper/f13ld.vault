# ScaffoldVault

A community-powered metamaterial structure library and recommendation engine. Describe your application requirements — the tool finds the geometry.

**[Open ScaffoldVault](https://mshomper.github.io/scaffold-vault)**

---

## What It Is

ScaffoldVault is a searchable database of TPMS (Triply Periodic Minimal Surface) metamaterial designs generated by the [TPMS Parameter Sweep Tool](https://mshomper.github.io/metamaterial-sweep-tool). Each record contains full mechanical and thermal homogenization results alongside the complete implicit geometry definition needed to reconstruct the surface in the [TPMS Builder](https://mshomper.github.io/tpms-builder).

The goal is simple: an engineer who needs a bone scaffold, a heat exchanger, or a lightweight aerospace component shouldn't need to understand TPMS mathematics. They describe what they need. ScaffoldVault finds the structure.

---

## How to Use

1. Select your **application domain** — Biomedical, Aerospace, Automotive, Oil & Gas, or Thermal
2. Set **target requirements** — metric ranges relevant to your application (microstrain, pore size, anisotropy, etc.)
3. Adjust **importance weights** for each requirement
4. Click **Find Structures** — results are ranked by match score
5. Click any result to inspect the full metric set and preview the surface geometry
6. Click **Get Recipe JSON** to download the design file
7. Load the recipe into the [TPMS Builder](https://mshomper.github.io/tpms-builder) to visualize, modify, and export for printing
